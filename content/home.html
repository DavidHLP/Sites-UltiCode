For the people making the decision

Good questions.
Before big commitments.

A useful platform is only part of the decision. Start with the requirements your IT, procurement and academic teams need to agree on.

Identity and access

Map who needs access, who manages it, and which institutional sign-in requirements apply. Review the project’s authentication reference before choosing a pilot.

To confirm: identity provider fit, roles and account lifecycle.

Read the authentication reference ↗
Data and institutional policies

Agree where learner and submission data will live, who can access it, and when it should be retained or deleted. Evaluate your chosen deployment against your institution’s policies.

To confirm: hosting location, retention, export and deletion procedures.

Deployment and ownership

Review the open-source project and its deployment guidance with the team that will run it. Production infrastructure, configuration and operational ownership belong in that plan.

To confirm: environment fit, operating team and upgrade process.

Read the operations guide ↗
Uptime and continuity

Set service targets for teaching and assessment periods. Review monitoring, backups and recovery with your operating team before relying on a deployment.

To confirm: availability targets, recovery procedures and incident ownership. No uptime SLA is offered on this site.

Support and long-term fit

Explore the documentation and public issue tracker, then decide what support your institution needs. Confirm responsibility for training, maintenance and incidents before rollout.

To confirm: support arrangements and response expectations. No response-time commitment is offered here.

A considered start

Start small.
Decide with evidence.

A pilot should answer your questions, with a clear owner and an agreed decision at the end. Set the pace that fits your institution.

  1. 01

    Define the scope

    Choose one course or cohort. Agree what you want to learn and what success would look like.

  2. 02

    Review the essentials

    Bring IT and academic owners together to review identity, data, hosting and support.

  3. 03

    Run a focused pilot

    Try a defined set of activities. Gather feedback from instructors, students and operators.

  4. 04

    Choose what comes next

    Review the evidence. Decide whether to expand, adjust the plan or close the pilot.
