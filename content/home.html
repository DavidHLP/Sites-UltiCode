A considered start

Start small.
Decide with evidence.

A pilot should answer your questions, with a clear owner and an agreed decision at the end. Set the pace that fits your institution.

  1. 01

    Define the scope

    Choose one course or cohort. Agree what you want to learn and what success would look like.

  2. 02

    Review the essentials

    Bring IT and academic owners together to review identity, data, hosting and support.

  3. 03

    Run a focused pilot

    Try a defined set of activities. Gather feedback from instructors, students and operators.

  4. 04

    Choose what comes next

    Review the evidence. Decide whether to expand, adjust the plan or close the pilot.

Evaluation planning

Start with the
right brief.

Bring these questions to your academic and IT owners for a self-hosted evaluation.

  • Which course or practice workflow will you evaluate?
  • What decision and measurable criteria will end the evaluation?
  • Who owns identity, permitted data and operations?
  • How will you handle support, recovery and data removal?

Your team operates the evaluation. This planning tool does not reserve a service or submit a request. Read the protocol or download the decision worksheet.
